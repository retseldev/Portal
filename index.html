Three tools, one connected system

Everything a pre-school or elementary school needs to track, support and guide its learners, in one place.

Student attendance tracking

Mark learners present, late or absent in a few taps. Teachers see class history and guardians stay informed.

Peer tutoring matching

Pair learners who need a hand with peer tutors by subject and available time.

Academic advising

Request an appointment with a teacher and track its status without the back-and-forth.
